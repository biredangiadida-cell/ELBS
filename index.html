About ELBS

Everlasting Love Bible School (ELBS) is dedicated to equipping believers with Biblical knowledge, spiritual growth, and practical ministry training. Our mission is to prepare faithful servants of God through quality Bible education.

🎓 Vision

Building faithful servants of Christ through Biblical education.

📖 Mission

Teaching God's Word with Knowledge, Faith and Love.

🌍 Our Goal

To spread Biblical education across Ethiopia and beyond.
